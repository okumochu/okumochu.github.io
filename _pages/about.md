Hi, I am Chia-Fan Chu (禇家帆). I am a graduate student in Information Management at National Taiwan University, where I finish my master's degree in 2026 and continue as a PhD student with the same advisor at the Productivity Optimization Lab.

I work at the intersection of applied operations research and machine learning, with a focus on scheduling and combinatorial optimization under uncertainty. My master's research develops deep reinforcement learning methods for dynamic flexible job shop scheduling: heterogeneous graph neural networks for state representation, curriculum learning and reward shaping for training, and multi-criteria objectives such as makespan and total weighted tardiness. My broader interest is in learning-based methods that adapt to disturbances the way real shop floors do, rather than re-solving from scratch each time the world shifts.

Looking ahead, I am exploring how large language models can act as decision interfaces for manufacturing: translating a manager's natural-language intent into decision contracts that drive scheduling systems, so that optimization tools get adopted instead of admired.
